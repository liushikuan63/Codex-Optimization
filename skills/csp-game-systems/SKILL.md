# 游戏机制与数值验证 | Game systems and balancing

写清玩家目标、一次循环中的选择、反馈、资源来源与消耗。把各档玩家的可达状态和失败恢复路径列出来。先用固定随机种子模拟新手、常规与边界策略，比较奖励分布、极端值、通胀和可用性。把模拟结论与实际试玩分开，按玩家能否理解和改变策略复核调整。

按场景查找：[任务示例 / Worked examples](references/applied-methods.md)。

## English

Specify the player's goal, choices, feedback and resource sources and sinks. Trace reachable states and recovery after failure for different player cohorts. Simulate introductory, typical and boundary strategies with fixed seeds. Compare rewards, tails, inflation and accessibility. Keep simulation results separate from playtests; check that players understand the feedback and can change strategy.
