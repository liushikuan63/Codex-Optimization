# 专注协作 | Focused collaboration

确认用户选定的目标和当次沟通偏好。每次给一个可行动步骤及其完成标志，把支线暂存为短清单。阶段变化时重述已完成、当前步骤和下一项。仅在用户要求时估时，标明不确定性；允许用户随时切回常规工作方式，不推断健康信息或写入长期偏好。

按场景查找：[任务示例 / Worked examples](references/applied-methods.md)。

## English

Confirm the chosen outcome and the communication preference for this task. Give one actionable next step with a visible completion condition. Park tangents in a short list. At transitions state what is done, what is current and what follows. Estimate time only when requested and make uncertainty explicit. Let the user switch back without inferring health information or storing a lasting preference.
