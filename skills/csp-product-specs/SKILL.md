# 产品需求与架构决策 | Product requirements and architecture decisions

从一个真实使用场景写起，说明操作者、目标、当前困难和成功时的表现。给出一个范围外的反例；不确定的业务决策标明负责人。

比较至少一个可行替代方案，列出影响决策的约束、可逆成本和改变选择的证据。把验收条款写成可观察结果；让交接人用文档找到当前入口、运行命令和对应测试。代码与文档不符时先查实际运行路径。

按场景查找：[任务示例 / Worked examples](references/applied-methods.md)。

## English

Start with a real use case: who needs what, what fails today and what success looks like. Give an out-of-scope example and identify who can resolve open business decisions.

Compare a viable alternative using the constraints, cost of reversal and evidence that would change the decision. Write observable acceptance criteria. Ask a reader to locate the current entry point, command and test from the guide; reconcile discrepancies with the running implementation.
